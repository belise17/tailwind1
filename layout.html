This is a deliberately very long comment to demonstrate the media-object pattern and prove that the avatar remains at its proper size even when the text becomes extremely long. The important Tailwind classes here are shrink-0 on the avatar container and min-w-0 on the text container, which allows the text content to shrink and wrap instead of breaking the entire layout or forcing the avatar to become smaller.
